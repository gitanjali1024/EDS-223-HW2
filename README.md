# Comparing Environmental Justice and Collision data across redlining HOLC grades

This repository conveys information on environmental justice indicators and bike/pedestrian crashes across 4 HOLC grades in Los Angeles. Redlining in Los Angeles still has effects to this day, and this document looks at income, air pollution and life expectancy, as well as how worse infrastructure in these redlined areas has caused significanty more bike and pedestrian accidents.

## References
The ejcreen data comes from the the US EPA EJScreen data. The original data no longer exists, but an unofficial copy can be found [here](https://pedp-ejscreen.azurewebsites.net/). 

The HOLC redlining data comes from a team of researchers at the Digital Scholarship Lab and can be found [here](https://dsl.richmond.edu/panorama/redlining/#loc=5/39.1/-94.58)

The bike and pedestrian crash data comes from the Statewide Integrated Traffic Records System and is hosted on the Transportation Information Mapping System hosted by UC Berkeley. It can be found [here](https://tims.berkeley.edu)
